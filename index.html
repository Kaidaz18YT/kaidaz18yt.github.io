About me

I'm Kaidaz18, a small Minecraft Bedrock creator working on growing an audience through add-ons and tutorials. My main focus is Minecraft Education Edition — building fixes and utilities for problems that edition-specific players run into, and making videos that walk through how to install and use them.

So far I've published three add-ons on CurseForge: an Entity Counter for tracking mobs on a farm, a Crosshair Fix for a long-standing Education Edition bug, and a Different Coloured Chickens pack for a bit of variety in the creative menu. Between them they've picked up over 1,700 downloads.

I'm currently working on longer-form content — the kind of video meant to be sat down and watched, rather than just a quick tutorial — alongside the shorter install guides and how-tos I already make.
