Processing happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Each video is decoded frame-by-frame (not played back live) so the output isn't affected by playback stutter, then combined with the original audio and packaged as .mp4. The first run downloads a small conversion engine, which is cached after that and makes the first trim slower than the rest.
