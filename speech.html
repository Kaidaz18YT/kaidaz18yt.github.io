How to use it

About ten seconds to get going.
  1. Allow the microphone when your browser asks. Nothing is saved or sent to this website.
  2. Press Start dictation and speak normally. The button turns red while it is listening.
  3. Press it again to stop. Press it once more to add more text to the same note.
  4. Hit Copy text and paste it wherever you need it.

This is handy for writing on a tablet, for long notes you would rather speak than type, and for students who find typing slow or tiring. It runs on the same browser speech recognition that powers voice search, so it needs an internet connection — but the audio goes to your browser's own service, not to this site.

Questions

The short answers.
Is this dictation tool free?

Yes. Voice Notes is free, has no account and no word limit of its own. It uses the speech recognition that is already built into your browser.

Does it record my voice?

The page never stores or uploads audio. Your browser passes the speech to its speech recognition service — Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari — and only the text comes back to this page.

Which browsers support it?

Chrome, Edge and Safari on desktop and Android, plus Safari on iOS. Firefox does not support the Web Speech API yet, so the page will tell you to try another browser.

Can I use it offline or on a school Chromebook?

It works on a Chromebook in Chrome, but speech recognition needs an internet connection because the browser sends the audio to its own recognition service. Nothing is sent to this website.
