FAQ

 

Do you need my hosting password?

No, never. Most hosts (Apex, Shockbyte, BisectHosting, PebbleHost…) let you add a sub-user. You add me as one, and I only get access to the server panel, not your billing or account. You can remove me when we're done. I'll walk you through it on Discord.

What if it breaks later?

If something I set up breaks within a few days because of something I did, I'll fix it free. After that, or if it broke because of a new plugin, mod or update, it's a normal $10 fix.

I don't know if I need Paper, Fabric or Forge.

Pick "Not sure". Roughly: Paper is for plugins (ranks, claims, economy) and players join with normal Minecraft. Fabric and Forge are for mods, and every player needs the same mods installed. Tell me what you want and I'll recommend one.

Can you set up a server on my own PC?

Yes, but I can't log in to your computer. I'll send you the files and step-by-step instructions on Discord, and help you over call or chat if you get stuck.
